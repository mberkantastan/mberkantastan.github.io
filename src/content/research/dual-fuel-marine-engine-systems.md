## Research objective

This research examines the supporting systems required to operate a low-speed dual-fuel marine engine safely and reliably with gaseous fuel.

The goal is to understand each subsystem well enough to represent its operational logic in engineering simulation software.

## Main topics

- LNG bunkering and storage
- gas fuel supply architecture
- pilot fuel systems
- boil-off gas management
- gas valve units and double-wall piping
- ventilation and gas detection
- emergency shutdown logic
- operating sequences and interlocks

## Simulation perspective

For simulation work, a system drawing alone is not sufficient. The model also needs state transitions, permissives, trips, alarms, valve logic, pressure and temperature relationships, and realistic operator procedures.

Future entries will break these systems into smaller technical studies with references, diagrams and implementation notes where publication is appropriate.
