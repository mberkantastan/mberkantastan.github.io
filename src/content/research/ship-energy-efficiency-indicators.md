## Overview

This research area focuses on turning ship energy-efficiency indicators into dynamic engineering calculations that can be used inside simulation and digital-twin environments.

Instead of treating efficiency metrics only as static reporting values, the objective is to connect them with changing operating conditions such as propulsion load, machinery demand, vessel speed and voyage profile.

## Indicators and topics

- Energy Efficiency Existing Ship Index (EEXI)
- Energy Efficiency Design Index (EEDI)
- Energy Efficiency Operational Indicator (EEOI)
- Carbon Intensity Indicator (CII)
- Specific Fuel Oil Consumption (SFOC)
- voyage and operational efficiency analysis
- reporting and scenario comparison

## Engineering direction

A simulation-based approach makes it possible to investigate how operational decisions affect fuel consumption and emissions before those decisions are applied in the real world.

Future work will include calculation notes, data-processing methods, model assumptions and selected visualizations suitable for public technical documentation.
