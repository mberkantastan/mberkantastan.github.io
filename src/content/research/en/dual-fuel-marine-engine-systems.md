## Research objective

The purpose of this work is to build a technically structured understanding of the auxiliary systems required around a low-speed dual-fuel propulsion engine and to translate that understanding into simulator-ready system definitions.

## Main research blocks

- LNG bunkering and storage architecture.
- Gas fuel supply and conditioning.
- Pilot-fuel arrangements.
- Boil-off-gas management.
- Double-wall piping, ventilation, gas detection and shutdown logic.
- Valve, permissive, alarm and interlock relationships.

## Simulator perspective

For simulation work, a P&ID alone is not sufficient. Each system must be decomposed into states, manipulated variables, measured variables, limits, failure modes and transition conditions. That structure is what eventually allows the system to be tested in a repeatable scenario.
