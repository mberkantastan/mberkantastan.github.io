## Objective

This research connects regulatory and operational energy-efficiency indicators with dynamic machinery and voyage data so that efficiency can be examined as a time-dependent engineering problem rather than a static calculation.

## Indicators and data

The work covers EEXI, EEDI, EEOI, CII, SEEMP-related operational thinking and SFOC-based machinery assessment. The analytical layer can use machinery load, RPM, fuel consumption, route and environmental variables as inputs.

## Digital-twin value

A digital-twin-oriented simulator can expose how operational decisions change fuel consumption and emissions over a voyage profile. This makes efficiency metrics useful for training, scenario comparison and engineering analysis.
